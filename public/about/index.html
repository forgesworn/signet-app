What is MySignet?

Manage your identities and sign in to compatible apps. Recognisable npubs, control over signing and clear app permissions.

Choose where your key lives

Keep signing keys encrypted on this device, or use a connected browser extension or remote signer. MySignet shows each identity's public address (npub) so you can recognise which account will sign.

When a site shows Sign in with Signet, you approve the request and you're in. Sites and relays can still see public keys and connection metadata. Separate personas help keep different uses of your identity apart.

Built for families

Guardians can manage a child's identity and control which requests need approval. Access and activity visibility depend on the family's settings.

Change permissions as the child's needs grow. Review connected apps and signing permissions together.

Trust that adds up

MySignet is built on Signet, an open protocol for identity verification. You start self-declared, gain standing as people vouch for you, and can be verified by licensed professionals such as solicitors and notaries rather than by any central authority.

Age proofs can confirm an age threshold without sharing your birth date. Documents saved in MySignet are encrypted locally; public identifiers and some record metadata remain readable. What a verifier or app learns depends on the proof and information you share.

What to expect

  • Configured services. Signet connects to relays and optional media services. Encryption does not hide every public event field or network connection.
  • Nostr identities. Use npubs across compatible apps. Keep your recovery words and separate backups for imported accounts.
  • Android and web. Camera and biometric features depend on your device and browser's support.
