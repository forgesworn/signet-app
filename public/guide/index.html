The basics

Four ideas, and you know how it works

MySignet looks after a few keys for you. Each has a job. None of them lives on someone else's server.

Persona

Your everyday face. The name you choose when you start. It never carries your legal name, and at most it can show an age range. It's the default for everything, sign-ins included.

Real identity

Asleep until you need it. It's created alongside your persona but stays switched off until you activate it. You only need it for verification, venue entry, documents and professional features. Family doesn't need it.

Recovery words

12345678910111213141516171819

19 words, in order, on paper. Words 1 to 7 are a format header, so they look much the same on every backup. Words 8 to 19 are your key. Write down all 19.

Keys stay with you

No server. No account. Everything is encrypted on your device and opens with your fingerprint, face or PIN. Backups to your other devices are encrypted before they leave.

1

Most people

Start with the app

A phone and a piece of paper. That's the whole setup.

  1. Install MySignet

    On Android, get the app from mysignet.app/get. On iPhone or a computer, open mysignet.app in your browser.

  2. Tap Create my Signet

    This makes your keys on the device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  3. Pick a persona name

    A name or handle. It's what sites and contacts see. Tap Continue.

  4. Lock it

    On Protect your Signet, choose Use fingerprint or face where your device supports it (in the Android app, Android 11 and later), or Use a 6-digit PIN.

  5. Write down your 19 words

    Go to Settings → Security & Backup → Backup Words. Copy all 19 onto paper, in order. Keep the paper somewhere safe.

  6. Optional: share the load

    Under Shamir Backup, tap Manage Shamir Backup to split your backup into 3 shares for people you trust. Any 2 of the 3 bring it back.

  7. You're ready

    Your persona card sits on Home. Contacts, Bunker and Settings are along the bottom.

2

Bring what you have

I already have a Signet

On the welcome screen, tap I already have a Signet. Then pick whichever of these you hold. After that, you lock the app just as in path 1.

  • Your recovery words

    Tap Enter my recovery words and type your 19 words (31 for a longer key). Got an older 12-word backup? Tap I have an older 12-word backup first.

  • A Heartwood or another signer

    Tap Connect a remote signer and paste its bunker:// link. Path 3 walks through it.

  • A Signet Lite phrase

    Tap Restore from Signet Lite and enter the phrase from Signet Lite.

  • A guardian's pairing code

    Setting up a child's phone? Tap Pair with my guardian's phone and follow the code your guardian shows you.

  • A Nostr key

    Already on Nostr? Tap Import a Nostr key (nsec / extension), then paste your nsec or connect your browser extension.

3

The gold standard

Keys born on a Heartwood

Heartwood is ForgeSworn's open-source hardware signer: firmware for small ESP32 boards such as the Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 (V3 or V4). Your master secret is made on the device and stays there. Your phone asks, the device signs.

Heartwood is in beta. Right now it suits people who are happy to flash firmware onto a board. If that isn't you yet, start with the app. You can move your keys across later (path 4).

  1. Set up the Heartwood with Sapwood

    Open Sapwood in Chrome or Edge and plug the board in over USB.

  2. Write your words from the device

    The Heartwood makes your key and shows your recovery words on its own screen. They never pass through your phone.

  3. Connect MySignet

    Tap I already have a Signet → Connect a remote signer. Paste the bunker:// link, pick a persona name and tap Connect.

  4. Approve on the Heartwood

    Hold the device's button to accept. It may ask more than once; on newer firmware it's one long hold.

  5. Bring over the operator key

    Sapwood gives you an operator key. Add it under Settings → Advanced → Heartwood operator key, so you can set family rules from your phone.

  6. Carry on as normal

    New personas and dependants are now made on the device. Only public keys come back to the phone.

One honest trade-off. Two things need a key held on the phone, so they aren't available in hardware mode: adding a family member with a shared secret, and the in-person ceremony for verifying someone else.

Source and boards: github.com/forgesworn/heartwood-esp32

4

App now, Heartwood later

Upgrade when you're ready

Start on the phone today. When you get a Heartwood, a wizard moves your keys (and your family's) onto it. It checks every key before it removes anything.

  1. Back up your words first

    The wizard won't start until your backup is done. Without it you'll see Back up your recovery words first.

  2. Put your words on the Heartwood

    Type your recovery words into the Heartwood, so it holds the same key as your phone.

  3. Open the wizard

    Settings → Advanced → Migrate family to Heartwood → Start migration. It lists who moves: you, and your family.

  4. Connect and let it check

    Paste the Heartwood's bunker:// link. The device rebuilds each key from your words, and the wizard checks every one is identical to the key on your phone.

  5. Only then, keys leave the phone

    When everything matches and you confirm your words are written down, it clears the keys from the phone. If any key doesn't match, nothing is removed.

Same you afterwards. Your public keys don't change, so your contacts and sign-ins carry on as before. Keys you imported from elsewhere stay on the phone, and the wizard lists them under Stays on this phone.

Family

Look after the people you look after

Children get their own keys, held by you until they're ready to take them. You don't need to switch on your real identity for any of this.

Add a dependant

From the Add card on Home, tap Add someone you look after (or, once you have a family, Settings → Family → + Add dependant), then enter their name and, if you like, their date of birth. You hold their keys, which come from your own recovery words. One day they take them with them.

Pair their phone

On their page, under Phone & Pairing, tap Pair a device and check the words match on both phones. With a Heartwood, their phone pairs straight to it.

Rules and asks

You decide what needs your say-so. When they ask for something new, it comes to you in the Bunker tab to approve or deny. With a Heartwood, these appear under Family asks.

Your contact manager

A contact book that knows it's them

MySignet keeps the people you know alongside their keys, so you can tell the real person from a lookalike. Your contacts are yours: kept on your device and backed up, encrypted, to your other devices.

Meet in person: Handshake

Scan their QR or, in the Android app, tap the phones back to back. With Bluetooth on, it can even work without the internet.

Scan a QR code

A MySignet card or any Nostr profile QR (an npub) adds them straight away.

Bring your Nostr follows

Tap Import following to add the people you already follow on Nostr.

Check it's really them

After a request is accepted, you each see a few words: read them to each other and tap Confirm their words. For anyone else, Confirm it's them lets you scan their QR or read out the end of their key on a call.

Share your name and photo, if you like

When you send or accept a request, They'll see lets you choose what goes with it. Their pictures are only downloaded when you ask: when you import follows, or with Refresh pictures.

Block, honestly

Blocking works inside Signet. It can't stop someone posting on Nostr or reaching you through an unrelated app.

Sign in everywhere

No password. No email. Just approve.

Where you see Sign in with Signet, MySignet does the rest. You choose which persona the site sees.

  1. Tap Sign in with Signet

    On the same phone, MySignet opens. On a computer or TV, a QR code appears: scan it with your phone.

  2. Choose who signs in

    Under Sign in as, your persona is picked for you. Your real identity only appears if you've switched it on, and the site asked for it.

  3. Tap Approve

    You're in. Nothing to remember, nothing to leak.

  4. Stay in charge

    Settings → Connections opens Connected Sites: every site you've signed in to. Remove one and it has to ask you again.

Games on the big screen

Some games show a QR on the TV or computer. Approve on your phone, and your phone offers a button to open the game's own page, which can be its controller.

KithMoot

A workspace nobody owns: rooms, files and calls over Nostr, across all your devices. Sign in with Nostr, using MySignet.

kithmoot.forgesworn.dev

Kinterest

A shared family pocket-money ledger that parent and child both see. No card, no real money. Guardians sign in with MySignet.

Nostr apps on your Android phone

Apps such as Amethyst can ask MySignet to sign for them (NIP-55). You approve, and you choose the key. Your real identity is never offered while it's switched off.

Nostr apps on your computer

Open the Bunker tab and Turn the Bunker on, then pair the app with the Bunker URL under Settings → Bunker & Scanning, or by scanning its nostrconnect:// code.

Always on

In the Android app, Always on (background) keeps the Bunker answering with the screen off, and notifies you when something needs your approval.

When you need your real identity

Wake it up only when it matters

It isn't used for ordinary sign-ins. A site only ever sees it if you pick it yourself and confirm.

Activate it

Type your legal name, then type it again to confirm, and tap Activate my real identity. From then on, that name is what verifiers, venues and your family see.

Get verified

Visit a verifier: your GP, solicitor, teacher, accountant or another registered professional. They check your ID in person and you receive the credential on your phone.

Venue entry

Your venue entry pass is a signed QR code that refreshes every 30 seconds, so an old screenshot won't get anyone in.

Good habits

Six small habits, a lot of peace of mind

  • Words on paper, never on a screen. Write all 19 in order. Don't photograph them or keep them in notes, email or cloud storage.

  • Lost your phone? You haven't lost you. Install MySignet on a new phone, tap I already have a Signet → Enter my recovery words.

  • Persona first. Sign in with your persona. Keep your real identity for the moments that need it.

  • Tidy your sign-ins. Look through Settings → Connections now and then, and remove sites you no longer use.

  • Confirm new contacts. Check it's really them, in person or with words, before you trust a new key.

  • Stay up to date. The bottom of Settings shows your version and tells you when a new one is out.
